## Why it exists

Three frontends drift apart quickly without shared tokens. Accessibility is also easier to get right once, in shared
components, than three times. The components are owned code (generated by the shadcn-svelte CLI and then restyled),
so the look is Colophon's and nothing upstream can change it without a reviewed diff.

## End goal

One token file and one library of owned components that every frontend uses, with light, dark and high-contrast
schemes, accessibility built in and tested, and the maintainer's theme, shared with the website and the docs: white paper, quiet cards, one clear blue, and statuses
that each mean one thing.

## Responsibilities

- Design tokens as CSS custom properties from one source, `design/tokens.css` (a byte copy in `src/lib/tokens.css`,
  checked), mapped into Tailwind's theme (`src/lib/styles.css`); the contrast table (`docs/design/contrast.md`) is
  generated from it and checked.
- Owned Svelte components (`src/lib/components/ui/*`), exported as `@colophon/ui/ui/<name>`.
- Accessibility defaults: focus rings, contrast, reduced motion, target sizes.

## Not its job

- Page layouts. Each app owns its own navigation and layout (the Studio's shell lives in `apps/studio/src/lib/shell`).
- Content rendering ([content-renderer](12-content-renderer.md)).
- The public blog's visual identity. The blog may use these tokens, but its look is its own
  ([web-blog](15-web-blog.md)).

## Breakdown

### Colour

White paper (`--background`, `--card`, `--popover`) and near-black ink (`--foreground`) in OKLCH, on the maintainer's
neutral ramp, with hairline rules (`--border`) and form-field boundaries at 3:1 (`--input`, WCAG 1.4.11). One accent,
blue: `--primary` (a shade deeper than the theme's pasted blue, so white labels read 5.26:1) fills the one primary
button on a screen (`--primary-hover` under the pointer); `--brand`, the pasted blue, marks the selection and is the
focus ring (`--ring`); `--link` and `--brand-ink` are the accent as text (lighter in dark mode, where the deeper blue
can't be read on a card) and `--brand-soft` its ground. It is never a surface. Five status tones, each as four tokens (a mark
for a dot or an icon, a soft ring around it, a soft ground for its tag, and an ink that reads on that ground):

| Tone | Colour | Meaning in the Studio |
|---|---|---|
| `live` | green | readers have it: live, new in a release, published, approved, verified |
| `changed` | violet | live, with changes not yet published; updated in a release |
| `pending` | amber | on its way: scheduled, in review, a website update pending |
| `attention` | rose | needs a person (stale, changes asked for, needs attention, not published) or taken down |
| `draft` | grey | not published, not sent for review, cancelled |

Colour never carries a state alone: every tag has an icon and a word. The accent sits at least 30° of hue from every
status, so it never reads as one. `--control` is the edge of a checkbox or radio (3:1 against what it sits on),
`--destructive` and `--destructive-foreground` the one destructive button. Five chart colours lead with the accent.
Dark mode uses the theme's neutral greys, cards a step lighter; the primary button keeps its blue with white text, and
accent text uses the lighter `--link`. Under
`prefers-contrast: more` text, inks, rules and the accent darken (or lighten, in dark mode) by themselves; there is no
contrast setting to find.

### Type, shape, elevation, motion

Two faces. Everything people read and use, page titles included, is Inter with its optical-size axis. Geist Pixel
(`--font-title`, the landing's own latin subset, sha256 in `design/tokens.css`) names the product: the wordmark and
empty-state titles, never forms or menus; it loads only where it's used. A page's intro under its title is one
line (`.page-intro`). Figures are tabular (`.figure`, `.tabular`); small labels are `.eyebrow`. No text is smaller than
14px: the theme sets `text-xs` to 14px, the same as `text-sm`, so small text is set apart by colour and weight, not size.
Mono (the system's monospace, nothing downloaded) only for hashes, ids, addresses and code. Shape: the theme's radius
scale (1.15rem; sm/md/lg/xl at -4px, -2px, 0, +4px), `--radius-control` (a step under) for buttons, fields,
toggles and segmented controls, `--radius-card` (a step over) for cards and dialogs, and pills only for status tags, badges
and switches; a card's padding is `--card-padding` (24px), or `--card-padding-sm` (16px) when dense (`p-card`,
`rounded-card`, `rounded-control`). Elevation: buttons and cards are flat on a hairline; only what floats over a page
casts a shadow (`shadow-float`), and a raised segment a `shadow-hairline`. Motion is quick and purposeful and is cut to
nothing under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Components

Every shadcn-svelte component (64 with Colophon's toolbar), generated by its CLI and owned here. The registry's "sera"
style is swept out (no uppercase, no wide tracking, no underline-only fields), and the core set is restyled by hand.
Buttons form one hierarchy: `default` is the primary, in the accent, one per screen; `secondary` (and `outline`, the same)
for everything else; `ghost` in toolbars and rows; `destructive` for a confirmation's last step; `link` inside a
sentence. Each button carries `data-variant`, so a test can count the primaries on a screen. Dialogs are bounded by the
screen: the body scrolls, the footer sticks to the bottom edge, and the title is in the display face. Checkboxes and
radios have a `--control` edge and take the accent when chosen; native `<input type="checkbox">` and `"radio"` are styled
the same in the base layer. Badges keep `live`, `changed`, `pending`, `attention`, `draft` and `brand` variants for
labels that aren't a state; a state is the Studio's one `StatusBadge` (`apps/studio/src/lib/shell/StatusBadge.svelte`,
its states in `lib/status.ts`). `cn` knows the custom shadows, shapes and card padding, so a caller's `shadow-none`,
`rounded-xl` or `p-4` replaces a component's own. Translucent text colours are not used, because the contrast test can
only check tokens. Every token, the status palette, the badge and every component are on one page: `/studio/design`.

## Invariants

| ID | Invariant |
|---|---|
| U1 | Every interactive component is keyboard-operable, labelled and visibly focused. |
| U2 | Motion respects the reader's reduced-motion setting. |
| U3 | Text uses token colours only, so every text colour is covered by the contrast test. Text on a translucent ground (`bg-destructive/10`, `bg-primary/5`, `bg-muted/50`) is measured as that tint blended over each surface it sits on; a new tint with text on it gets a row in `scripts/design-contrast.mjs`. |
| U4 | Every text colour meets WCAG AA on every ground it is drawn on, and AAA under more contrast. |
| U5 | The stylesheet and fonts load from the app's own origin; nothing from another. |

## Interfaces

- `@colophon/ui/styles.css`: the tokens, Tailwind and the fonts. An app imports it and adds
  `@source "<path to packages/ui/src>"`, because Tailwind doesn't scan dependencies.
- `@colophon/ui/ui/<component>` and `@colophon/ui/utils` (`cn` and prop helpers).

## Failure modes

| Situation | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| A token change weakens contrast | `packages/ui/test/tokens.test.ts` fails and names the pair |
| A component uses a colour that isn't a token | Caught by axe in the Studio's design tests (it was, once: an inactive tab at 60% opacity) |
| An upstream library (Bits UI, shadcn-svelte) changes | Versions are pinned and the components are copies, so updates are reviewed diffs |

## Tests and evidence

- `packages/ui/test/tokens.test.ts`: WCAG contrast computed from the OKLCH values (from first principles, not by the
  rendering library) for every text-on-ground pair, in light and dark at AA and in both under more contrast at AAA,
  including the primary's label on its fill and hover, every status ink and the plain ink on every status ground; the
  focus ring and the checkbox edge at 3:1; the accent one hue and clear of the statuses; each status tone's four tokens;
  and no stylesheet loads from another origin. `packages/ui/test/cn.test.ts`: the custom shadows, shapes and padding merge.
- The Studio's design tests (`apps/studio/tests/design.spec.ts`): axe on the playground and on `/studio/design` in light,
  dark and both contrast schemes, the toolbar's keyboard model, the theme toggle (following the system until pressed,
  then surviving a reload), the contrast preference taking effect, page titles in Inter with neither DM Sans nor JetBrains Mono loaded, every status badge's icon,
  word and ground, flat buttons with the accent primary, a dialog on a phone (bounded, scrolling, footer at the edge,
  fields stacked), chosen checkboxes and radios in the accent, and no requests to other origins.
- `apps/studio/tests/server/screens.spec.ts`: home, overview, the articles list, the editor, a publish request and a
  receipt, in a project with every status, checked with axe and for at most one primary button at 1440×900 and 390×844
  in light and dark; with `STUDIO_SHOTS_DIR` it photographs them (`docs/screens/<screen>-<width>-<scheme>.png`).
- Screenshots of every Studio screen at desktop and phone widths, light and dark, for each design step.
