Slow lamb shoulder with harissa

Five minutes of work, five hours of oven. The kind of cooking that makes a flat smell like a restaurant.

Prep
15 min
Cook
300 min
Serves
6
Difficulty
A proper project

Ingredients

  • lamb shoulder, bone in2 kg
  • rose harissa4 tbsp
  • garlic cloves, crushed4
  • preserved lemon, finely chopped1
  • olive oil2 tbsp
  • red onions, wedged3
  • chickpeas, drained400 g tin
  • Greek yoghurt and corianderto serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 160°C (140°C fan). Mix the harissa, garlic, preserved lemon and oil into a paste.
  2. Score the lamb deeply and rub the paste all over, into the cuts. Season with salt.
  3. Put the onions in a roasting tin, sit the lamb on top, add a mug of water and cover tightly with foil.
  4. Roast for 4½ hours, then uncover, add the chickpeas and roast 30 minutes more until the lamb pulls apart with a fork.
  5. Rest 20 minutes, then pull the meat into the pan juices. Serve with yoghurt, coriander and flatbreads.
