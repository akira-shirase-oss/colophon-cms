Fig and almond tart

Frangipane is the most forgiving of the fancy fillings, and figs forgive even less than the pastry.

Prep
40 min
Cook
45 min
Serves
8
Difficulty
A proper project

Ingredients

  • sweet shortcrust pastry300 g
  • unsalted butter, soft100 g
  • caster sugar100 g
  • eggs2
  • ground almonds125 g
  • plain flour1 tbsp
  • ripe figs, quartered6
  • flaked almonds20 g
  • apricot jam, warmed2 tbsp

Method

  1. Line a 23 cm fluted tart tin with the pastry, chill 20 minutes, then blind-bake at 190°C (170°C fan) for 15 minutes.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the ground almonds and flour.
  3. Spread the frangipane in the case and press the figs in, cut side up. Scatter the flaked almonds.
  4. Bake 30–35 minutes until the frangipane is golden and springs back.
  5. Brush with the warmed jam while hot. Serve at room temperature, with cream if the day calls for it.
