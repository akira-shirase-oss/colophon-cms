Weeknight mushroom ragu

Deeply savoury, done in half an hour, and better the next day if any survives.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Serves
4
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • mixed mushrooms, sliced600 g
  • olive oil3 tbsp
  • onion, finely chopped1
  • garlic cloves, sliced3
  • tomato purée2 tbsp
  • red wine150 ml
  • chopped tomatoes400 g tin
  • dried rigatoni400 g
  • butter30 g
  • parmesan, gratedto serve

Method

  1. Sear the mushrooms in batches in hot olive oil until deeply browned; crowd them and they will stew.
  2. Soften the onion in the same pan for 8 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute more.
  3. Stir in the tomato purée, cook for a minute, then add the wine and let it bubble away by half.
  4. Add the tomatoes and a splash of water; simmer 12 minutes until thick. Season well.
  5. Cook the pasta a minute shy of the packet time, then finish it in the sauce with the butter and a ladle of pasta water.
