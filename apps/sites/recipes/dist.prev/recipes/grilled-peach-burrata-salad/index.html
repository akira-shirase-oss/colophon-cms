Grilled peach and burrata salad

August on a plate: charred fruit, cold cheese, hot honey. Ready before the barbecue warms up.

Prep
10 min
Cook
8 min
Serves
4
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • ripe peaches, halved and stoned4
  • burrata2 balls
  • olive oil2 tbsp
  • runny honey2 tbsp
  • chilli flakesa pinch
  • rocket80 g
  • toasted hazelnuts, chopped30 g
  • lemon juiceof half a lemon

Method

  1. Heat a griddle until smoking. Brush the peach halves with oil and grill cut-side down for 3–4 minutes until striped.
  2. Dress the rocket with lemon juice and a little oil, then pile onto a platter.
  3. Tear over the burrata, arrange the peaches, and drizzle with honey warmed with the chilli flakes.
  4. Scatter the hazelnuts, season with flaky salt and serve immediately.
