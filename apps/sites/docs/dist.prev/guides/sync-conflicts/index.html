Sync conflicts and how they resolve

Two devices recording the same notebook can never corrupt each other, because inserts are merged in order.

Because notes are insert-only, a "conflict" is just two appends. The log orders them by device timestamp, then device id.

The single genuinely conflicting case is two edits of one note on two devices: both land as revisions, and the later one wins the head pointer. Nothing is lost.
