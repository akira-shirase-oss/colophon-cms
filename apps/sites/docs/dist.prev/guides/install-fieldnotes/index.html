Getting started

Install Fieldnotes

Get the recorder on your device and signed in to your workspace in about five minutes.

Fieldnotes is a field-recording app: audio, coordinates and text, synced to your workspace when a network exists.

Before you start

  • A workspace you can sign in to

  • Ten minutes of connectivity for the first sync

Install it

  1. Download the build for your platform from the workspace’s Downloads page

  2. Open it and sign in with your workspace account

  3. Grant microphone and location permissions when asked

The first sync is the only one that needs a network. Everything after queues until you are back.
