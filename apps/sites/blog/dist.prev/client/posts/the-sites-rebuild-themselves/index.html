The sites now rebuild themselves

A website connection tells the site when a release is out. Publishing here now updates all five demo sites without anyone running a build.

Until today, publishing in Colophon and rebuilding the sites were two separate acts. The gap was easy to explain and easy to forget.

A website connection closes it: every release posts one webhook, the site answers 202, and the newest announced generation is built and promoted. Duplicate announcements cost nothing.
