Why every publish deserves a receipt

A publish button that only promises is a spinner, not a system. Here is what proof of publish actually looks like.

releasesediting

Most CMSs answer a publish with a spinner and, eventually, a green tick. The tick means the request finished, not that the site changed.

A receipt is different: it names the release, the destinations it was announced to, and the probes that went and looked.

What a receipt proves

  • The release committed, as a numbered generation

  • Each destination was told, with an idempotency key

  • A probe saw the generation marker on the live page

Read the Colophon-Generation header on any delivery response: it is the release your content came from.

Proof beats promises. A green tick is a mood; a receipt is evidence.

Every word on this site was published through Colophon as release 5 of the demo-sites project, and this page names the release it was built from.
