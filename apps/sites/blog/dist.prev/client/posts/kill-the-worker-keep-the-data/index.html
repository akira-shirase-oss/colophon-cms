Kill the worker, keep the data

The crash tests kill the delivery worker mid-POST on a loop. Nothing is lost and nothing happens twice; here is why.

engineering

Every external effect goes through an outbox row with an idempotency key. A killed job is claimed again under a new lease and its key is seen as already applied.

The invariant

  • No transaction spans an external call

  • Retried deliveries reuse one key

  • A deduplicating receiver applies exactly one effect

apply(key) if key not in applied:
    applied.add(key)
    really_apply()
