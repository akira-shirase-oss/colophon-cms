The quiet power of a preview deploy

Preview is where bad content goes to be caught. A fifteen-minute capability and a noindex header are all it takes.

workflow

The preview origin renders draft content under a capability that expires. It is noindex, no-store and locked to the one project.

An editor who can see the real page stops guessing. Review comments get shorter and better.
