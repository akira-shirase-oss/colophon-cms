Style sheets are infrastructure too

The fastest way to make a publication look careless is an inconsistent em rule. Centralise the choices once.

craft

A house style sheet is a dependency of every article. Treat it like code: reviewed, versioned, and enforced where it is cheap to enforce.

  • One spelling dictionary, not five

  • Numbers as words below ten, numerals above

  • An agreed name for every recurring thing
