Slug discipline for busy editors

Slugs are URLs, and URLs are promises. A short house rule keeps them stable without slowing anyone down.

craftediting

The slug is set once, at draft time, and then left alone unless someone is prepared to own the redirect.

  1. Write the title first, derive the slug once

  2. Never edit a slug after publish without a redirect

  3. Keep slugs under five words; drop articles

Boring rules, followed always, beat clever rules followed sometimes.
