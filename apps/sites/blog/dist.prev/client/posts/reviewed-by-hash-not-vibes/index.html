Reviewed by hash, not by vibes

A Colophon review approves a changeset hash. Edit anything afterwards and the approval no longer applies.

releasesworkflow

"Looks good to me" attached to a moving target is a meaningless approval. The review binds to the hash of exactly what was shown.

If the content changes after approval, the hash changes, and the publish refuses until someone approves the new hash.

Approving a diff you have not seen is not review; it is delegation.

Every word on this site was published through Colophon as release 5 of the demo-sites project, and this page names the release it was built from.
