Drafts are cheap, publish is forever

Insert-only revisions mean a draft can be rewritten a hundred times and still show its history. Publish is the point where history becomes a release.

workflowediting

A draft is a scratch space. Colophon keeps every revision anyway, because the cost of keeping them is small and the cost of losing one is a lost afternoon.

Publishing is different: the release pins exact revisions, and the pin is what readers get.

The line that matters

Drafts move. Releases do not. That single asymmetry is what makes a publish reviewable.

Every word on this site was published through Colophon as release 5 of the demo-sites project, and this page names the release it was built from.
