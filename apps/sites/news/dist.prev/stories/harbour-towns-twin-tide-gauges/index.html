Harbour towns vote to twin their tide gauges

Forty small harbours will pool readings into one public feed, ending a patchwork of incompatible local gauges.

Jonas Ferry, world desk · Filed 2026-11-02

Harbour masters from forty towns voted on Saturday to twin their tide gauges and publish the readings as a single public feed.

Each gauge keeps local control; the twin is a shared record sailors can read without visiting forty different websites.

The first unified feed is expected before the spring tides.
